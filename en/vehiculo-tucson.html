Maintenance and replacements included in the rent

01

Preventive maintenance

Mileage-based preventive maintenance from 5,000 km to 50,000 km.

  • Engine oil
  • Oil filter
  • Air filter
  • Fuel filter
  • Other consumables required at each service
  • Labour

The maintenance schedule and details are set out in Annex C (Anexo C) of the contract. The interval-by-interval details in Annex C are provided with the contract.

02

Major parts replacement

Under the maintenance plan, the following parts are replaced.

  • 30,000 kmBrake pads and disc resurfacing
  • 35,000 kmSuspension components
  • 40,000 kmDrive belt, wipers, bulbs and more

Scope and terms follow the contract and the maintenance plan.

03

Tyres and battery

  • Replacement of 4 tyres
  • Battery replacement
  • Interval: every 20,000 km

Terms follow the rental contract.

05

Municipal permit

We keep the municipal permit (Habilitación Municipal) valid for the whole contract.

06

Phone support

If you have a question or problem with the vehicle, we help by phone on weekdays and Saturdays during business hours.
